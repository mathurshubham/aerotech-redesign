Aircraft Recovery

We help our clients prepare internal teams and respond to identify potential issues with removing immobilized or disabled aircraft in a timely manner and meet the requirements set by ICAO for disabled aircraft operations. Our team provides emergency and disaster management services for all aviation stakeholder including Airline Operators, Airport Operator, Fueling Companies, Maintenance Hangars and other related critical utility infrastructure.

Our Services include:

delivering training and planning functions for aviation emergency or disaster events
safety inspections and critical infrastructure failure analyses
predictive and post incident impact monitoring and data collection
stakeholder engagement and management services following events
business contingency planning
incident command system (ICS) management
risk assessment & risk liability management
short-term and long-term action plan development for risk mitigation
