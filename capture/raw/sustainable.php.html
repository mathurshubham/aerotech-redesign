Sustainable Aviation Solutions

We help organizations ensure that they and their partners have efficient and manageable processes and systems in place which comply with stakeholder requirements related to quality, health and safety, environmental management, corporate social responsibility and other areas of sustainable development. With massive media coverage and huge public interest, finding ways to operate in a responsible manner is becoming increasingly important. we are assisting organizations to be kinder to the environment, have a positive impact on society and achieve greater economic sustainability.

The services are presently being offered to :-
1. AeroWash, Sweden
2. TaxiBot – India
3. Stelia
4. UPES, Dehradun
5. Robotic Disinfectant and Aircraft Sanitization System (UV-C)

CLIENT NAME : AEROWASH, SWEDEN
OPERATIONAL AIRPORT : New Delhi, Dubai,Kuala Lampur

Project Description : AeroTech Support Services had partnered with AEROWASH, a Swedish manufacturer for providing aircraft detailing services to Indian Carriers with use of latest technology aimed at enhancing airlines commitments towards greener projects whilst improving operational performance for its fleet.
Our team members have over 04 years of experience in successful implementation of Aerowash - Robotic Arircraft Exterior Dry-Wash Services for Airlines operating in INDIA, MALAYSIA and DUBAI Region. AeroTech Support Services hold special rights for implementing the solution in Middle East, Soth East Asia, INDIA and SARC Region. The Robotic cleaning aircraft Dry wash solution is first of its kind with Aerotech in-depth know how of the system results in smooth transition from water based non-measurable practice to more scientific approach towards cleaning aircraft, thereby resulting in measurable reduction in ATF consumption, Reduced Emissions and Zero water wastage while making the service available for airlines at parking stands.

CLIENT NAME : TAXIBOT-INDIA
OPERATIONAL AIRPORT : DELHI, BANGALORE, HYDERABAD AIRPORT

Project Description : AeroTech Support Services Had Been Instrumental In Developing Market For TaxiBot In India Which Includes Obtaining Regulatory Compliances, Approvals From OEM (Airbus And Boeing), Performing Operational Evaluation And Delivering A Final Product To Our Client.
“WORLD’S FIRST TAXIBOT OPERATIONS WITH AIRBUS (A321 Family) AND BOEING (B737-NG Family)”.

CLIENT NAME : STELIA
OPERATIONAL AIRPORT : New Delhi

Project Description : AEROTECH SUPPORT SERVICES HAD BEEN SELECTED TO SERVE AS FIELD REPRESENTATIVE TO STELIA AEROSPACE CLIENTELE IN INDIA. STELIA AEROSPACE IS A PIONEER IN MANUFACTURING OF BUSINESS CLASS SEATS FOR MOST REPUTED AIRLINES ACROSS THE GLOBE.

CLIENT NAME : UNIVERSITY OF PETROLEUM & ENEREGY STUDIES, INDIA
OPERATIONAL AIRPORT : Delhi

Project Description : AeroTech Support Services Had Partnered With UPES, Dehradun, A Premier Aviation Research Institute For Providing Research And Aviation Consulting To Students And Faculty Helping Them Understand The Dynamic Aviation Industry. We Are Working In Close Coordination With School Of Business On Organizing Industry First Online Knowledge Sharing Sessions For Students And Alumni.
