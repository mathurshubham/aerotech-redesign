Resource optimization Tool

Aero Opt facilitate the prediction of the passenger flow demand for planning purposes. Specially trained algorithms determine the resource requirements to achieve defined quality targets (Level of Service – LOS / Level of Health – LOH) for the passenger flow demand and enable an appropriate deployment of resources in order to minimize variable operational cost while still meeting quality targets.
