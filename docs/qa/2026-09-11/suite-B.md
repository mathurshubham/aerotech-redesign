## Fix list

**Blocker**
1. `/work/taxibot-india` (TaxiBot case study) — **missing PLACEHOLDER callout.** B4 requires one visible `[PLACEHOLDER…]` element on this page (per the artboard and the site's own placeholder pattern used elsewhere, e.g. footer CIN/GSTIN); a DOM text search found zero matches. Add the missing placeholder (likely a video/press-asset or additional-proof placeholder implied by the artboard) styled per the established mono/orange-border pattern.

**Major**
2. H1 sizing is inconsistent and exceeds the documented scale across pages — component/hook rendering the hero H1 style, used on `/` and `/work/taxibot-india`.
   - Observed: home H1 62px, TaxiBot H1 58px, ORAT H1 50px (all `font-weight: 700`).
   - Expected: `clamp(2.25rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)` → hard max **56px** at any desktop viewport ≥ ~1000px wide.
   - Fix: audit the H1/hero-heading Tailwind class(es) — check for a bespoke `text-[...]` override on the home/case-hero H1 that isn't going through the shared `clamp()` utility, since ORAT's H1 is correctly under the cap but home/TaxiBot are not.

**Minor**
3. `/services/orat`, "Who this is for" section — the section has no real H2; the eyebrow text ("Who this is for") is itself marked up as `<h2 class="eyebrow">` at 11px, unlike every other section on the same page which pairs an eyebrow with a full-size (28–38px) H2. Give this section its own heading text, or confirm intentionally omitted and adjust heading level instead of overloading the eyebrow tag.
4. Global — a light orange tint `#fff3ec` (appears 4× on home, likely a "New" pill or hover background) is not in the DESIGN.md token table. Either register it as a documented tint (`orange-50`/`surface-orange` or similar) or replace with an existing token at reduced opacity.
5. `/services/orat` engagement-shape panel shows 4 data rows (Engaged from / Phases / Deliverables / Led by) versus the artboard's 3 (Typical duration / Engaged from / Led by) — cosmetic content drift from the reference, worth a design sign-off but not a rule violation.

## Design verdict

The build reads convincingly as one system: navy/paper/white bands, mono eyebrows, hairline credential and deliverable grids, captioned photography, and the single 8px radius are applied consistently across home, ORAT, TaxiBot, About and Contact, and all five pages match their reference artboards band-for-band with no structural drift. Palette discipline holds — no leftover lime/green/blue, and orange stays confined to CTAs, eyebrows, numerals and rules. The two places it drifts from the drafts are: (1) the hero/case H1 does not consistently respect its own documented clamp — three pages, three different sizes, two of them over the ceiling — which is the kind of inconsistency that quietly erodes "one type scale" the longer it's copy-pasted into new pages; and (2) the TaxiBot case is missing its required PLACEHOLDER callout, a functional gap rather than a stylistic one. Neither undermines the overall system, but both should be fixed before this is called done.
