Operational Readiness & Transfer

A new terminal has one opening day and no rehearsal. ORAT is how you make the first flight look like the thousandth.

Engagement shape
Typical duration12–24 months
Engaged fromT−18 months
Led byA. Khanna
Who this is for

Airport operators

Opening a new terminal, a new runway, or a satellite concourse, with a fixed public opening date.

Airlines relocating

Moving a base or a hub operation between terminals without dropping an on-time performance target.

Ground handlers & fuellers

Standing up new equipment, new stands or new procedures alongside an operator’s transfer programme.

If your opening date is inside twelve months, the scope changes — there is no longer room for a full trials programme, and the work becomes risk triage. Say so on the call and we will scope it that way.

What you get

Deliverables, not a deck

Every item below is a document or an exercise your team owns at handover, and can run again next time without us.

01

ORAT master programme

Milestone plan from T−18 to T+3, dependency-mapped against construction and systems handover.

02

Trials & familiarisation plan

Scripted trials by system and by stakeholder, with volunteer numbers, scenarios and pass criteria.

03

Standard operating procedures

Terminal, apron and landside procedures written for the new facility, not adapted from the old one.

04

Passenger flow & LOS model

Demand forecast against IATA Level of Service targets, sized with Aero Opt. Drives staffing and counter allocation.

05

Transfer & cutover runbook

Hour-by-hour move plan, including the fallback position at every point where you can still turn back.

06

Day-one command structure

Who decides what during the stabilisation window, and the escalation path that keeps it off the CEO’s desk.

How it runs

Four phases against your opening date

T−18 → T−12

Readiness assessment

Baseline the facility, the org and the systems. Name every gap that has a long lead time, while it is still cheap.

T−12 → T−6

Procedures & training

Write the SOPs, build the training, model the flows. Staff learn the new building before they work in it.

T−6 → T−1

Trials

Progressive trials from single-system to full-scale with volunteer passengers. Every failure here is a free one.

T−1 → T+3

Transfer & stabilise

Run the cutover, hold the command structure through the stabilisation window, then hand it to your team.
